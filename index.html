24 hours later

I miss you.

My Katieee,

It has only been a day, but the silence already feels louder than I ever thought it would.

I spoke to your mum. I told her I didn’t think this would work. At that moment, I genuinely thought I was being rational. I thought I was making the difficult, sensible decision.

And then I actually had to sit with what that decision meant.

You not being in my life doesn’t feel like relief. It feels like a void I can’t even imagine learning to live with.

I know I was a little too dramatic. I overreacted. I let the emotion of one moment become bigger than everything we had built around it. That is on me. I’m not going to pretend otherwise.

You blocked me everywhere, and I understand why. I tried calling you on BOTIM, and when that door closed too, I understood that you wanted space. I’m going to respect that. I’m not going to keep finding new ways around a boundary you have clearly set.

But if you ever come back to this little website one day and happen to read this, there is something I want you to know.

I don’t just miss having someone to talk to. I don’t miss the routine. I don’t miss the attention. I miss you.

I miss your voice. I miss seeing your face suddenly appear on my screen and feeling stupidly excited every single time. I miss your expressions, your dramatic little moments, the random conversations, the softness, the stubbornness, and the way ordinary days somehow felt better just because you were somewhere inside them.

I had pictured December in Goa. Meeting your family properly. Being there not as some guy on a screen, but as someone standing beside you. I still hold that picture somewhere in my head — but only if you ever want that too.

I’m sorry for the part I played in getting us here. I know a website can’t undo what happened. It can’t erase hurt, and it shouldn’t be used to pressure you into anything.

So this isn’t me asking you to forget what happened. It’s simply me leaving the truth somewhere you might one day find it: if you ever choose to talk to me again, I’ll be here.

No argument. No ego. No trying to prove who was right. Just me listening properly this time.

And maybe — if life is kind to us — this becomes one of those ridiculous canon events we laugh about years from now.

“Remember that one time we broke up, blocked each other, and your idiot father turned an entire website into an apology?”

It is only a funny story if we earn the ending.

Until then, I won’t chase you. I won’t make you feel guilty. I won’t pretend your reaction wasn’t valid. I’ll respect the space you asked for.

But I will miss you.

And if there is ever a next time for us, I don’t want it to be temporary. I don’t want us to love each other only when everything is easy. I want us to choose each other properly — even on the difficult days.

Till I hear from you…
I’ll be right here.

Kev.
No buttons. No countdowns. No tricks.
Just something I should have said better the first time.

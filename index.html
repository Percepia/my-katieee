I even spoke to your mum and told her that I didn’t think this would work. And now I’m sitting here realizing how stupidly certain I was about something I clearly wasn’t ready to lose.

You not being in my life has left a void I genuinely cannot imagine getting used to.

I know you blocked me on WhatsApp. I tried BOTIM too, and later I was blocked there. You unfriended me on Snapchat, and we never followed each other on Instagram. I understand why you reacted the way you did.

But Katieee, if you ever come back to this website and see this message: please unblock me on WhatsApp and just drop me a text. Or ring me once on BOTIM. I will call you back.

I’m not asking you to pretend nothing happened. I’m asking for one more conversation — without the drama, without the ego, and without either of us throwing away something that mattered because of one bad moment.

And if we come back to each other this time, let’s stick together for life.

I meant what I said about us. I meant what I imagined for us. And if you still want me there, I will come down to Goa in December to meet your family properly.

Maybe this whole mess becomes our canon event. Maybe years from now we tell our children and grandchildren about the ridiculous time we broke up, blocked each other everywhere, and I ended up leaving a message for you on a website because I had finally realized what losing you actually felt like.

I don’t want this to be the end of our story.

So if you ever read this and there is even a little part of you that still wants us, you know where to find me.

Till I hear from you…
I miss you, Katieee.

Kev.
